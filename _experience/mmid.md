Spent a summer at a product design consultancy in Delft, which was a different kind of
engineering than the research I'd been doing — much more about client communication and
fast concept iteration. I worked on three fairly different concept-development projects in
parallel (a high-pressure pipe coupling, a chemical dilution testing apparatus, and an
electrical outlet wall box), relying a lot on hand-sketching to communicate ideas quickly,
including across a language/office culture that was new to me. I also ran mechanical tests
on wall-mounted clamp prototypes to verify our material choices actually held up to spec
before handing designs back to the client.
