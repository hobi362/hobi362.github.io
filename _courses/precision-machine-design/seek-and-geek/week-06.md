My first time fencing left me fascinated by the foil itself: when a hit lands, the blade
buckles and deflects — but critically, only upward. If it buckled downward instead, the tip
could slide up underneath the opponent's mask, which is a real safety failure, not just a
design nitpick. That's a nice example of a mechanism where the *direction* of an elastic
deflection is as much a design requirement as its magnitude.
