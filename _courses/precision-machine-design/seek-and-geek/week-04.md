A labmate uses ABB's YuMi — a collaborative, dual-arm small-parts-assembly robot — for his
research, and this was a good excuse to connect the homogeneous transformation matrices
(HTMs) we'd just covered in lecture to a real dual-arm system. YuMi is specifically designed
to work safely alongside a human at close range, which changes the precision-design
priorities compared to a caged industrial arm: compliance and force-limiting matter as much
as raw positioning accuracy.
