A contract role in medical device R&D, working on a peristaltic pump for hysteroscopic
procedures — minimally invasive surgery inside the uterus, where keeping intrauterine
pressure within a tight safe range is a real patient-safety constraint, not just a nice-to-have
spec. I also built assembly drawings in SolidWorks for handoff to packaging engineers for
large-scale manufacturing, and ran mechanical verification testing on surgical cutting
tools. This role led directly into full-time work at Medtronic once it wrapped up.
