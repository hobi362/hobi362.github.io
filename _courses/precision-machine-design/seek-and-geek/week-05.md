A V-brake looks simple until you actually trace through how the cable pull, pivot
locations, and pad geometry combine to turn a small hand-lever force into a large, evenly
applied clamping force on the rim — with the pivot placement doing most of the work of
keeping that mechanical advantage roughly constant through the brake's travel.
