My first real engineering internship, right after my sophomore year. I worked in the Fuel
Systems Group doing product validation — essentially, figuring out why parts fail and
whether a fix actually solves the root cause rather than just the symptom. Digging into
resonant frequency and surface finish as failure factors on high-pressure fuel lines was my
first real exposure to treating "why did this break" as a rigorous investigation rather
than a guess.
