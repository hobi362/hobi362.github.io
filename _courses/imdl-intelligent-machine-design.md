Capstone-style lab (technically cross-listed undergrad/grad) taught by Antonio Arroyo and
Eric Schwartz — the class where you design, build, and integrate an entire small autonomous
robot from scratch: microcontroller interfacing, analog/digital electronics, mechanical
design, and controls, all in one project.

My project was DrumBot, aimed at theme-park-style guest engagement (think: the video games
Disney adds to ride queues to fight boredom). DrumBot uses an Xbox Kinect to track a
person's "air drumming" motions, processes that motion on a laptop, and sends commands to a
BeagleBone Black, which fires solenoids mounted on a handmade drum kit to hit the
corresponding drums in real time. It also has basic autonomy for roaming — two motorized
wheels, a caster, and IR proximity/bump sensors to avoid obstacles while it's not actively
being played with.
