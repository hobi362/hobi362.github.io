After finishing my PhD, I stayed on at the d'Arbeloff Lab in a part-time consulting
capacity — helping newer lab members work through mechanical design problems on their own
robotic prototypes, the same way senior students had helped me. The gantry crane attachment
was a direct extension of my dissertation work: the same underconstrained-cable-suspension
ideas from my PhD, applied to a new crane setup manipulating a UR-5 robot arm.
