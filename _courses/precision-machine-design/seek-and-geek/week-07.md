A trip to Legoland's adult night turned into a study of Merlin's Apprentice, a ride where
kids pump a lever to control their own height — which means the mechanism has to be
forgiving of wildly inconsistent input forces and timing from small children while still
being mechanically safe and predictable. It's a good reminder that "precision" in a real
product is often about robustness to bad input as much as it is about tight tolerances.
