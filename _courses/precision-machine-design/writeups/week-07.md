This week was about finishing what Week 6 started: complete the fabrication of the linear
motion slide and run the stiffness, accuracy, and repeatability tests against the Week 6
predictions. I also deliberately kept notes on my own manufacturing process, since I knew
I'd need to build a second LMS over spring break for the lathe's other axis.

Two design changes came out of actually machining the part. The rail changed from an I-beam
to a pure T-beam, and the carriage — originally meant to be a single aluminum piece — became
two pieces of 25.4mm × 87mm × 101mm aluminum bolted and JB Welded together, which was far
more practical to actually machine accurately.

![The finished linear motion slide carriage](/assets/images/courses/precision-machine-design/week07-lms-finished.jpg)

I hadn't used a manual mill in a while, so the first pass was slow, but learning the CNC
features partway through sped things up considerably — and gave me a much better second
attempt when I rebuilt this same design for the lathe's other axis.
