On my first-ever ski lesson, I spent as much time studying the tow rope mechanism as I did
actually skiing. A rope tow has to grip a moving rope with enough friction to pull a skier
uphill, while letting riders grab on and let go smoothly at both ends — a simple-looking
system that's actually a neat friction and loading problem once you start asking how it
avoids slipping under a sudden grab or shredding the rope over thousands of cycles.
