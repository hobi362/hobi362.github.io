A summer at a product design consultancy in Delft, arranged through **MIT MISTI** — a
different kind of engineering from the research I'd been doing, much more about client
communication and fast concept iteration.

I worked on several concept-development projects in parallel, brainstorming ideas, sketching
and building the SolidWorks designs:

- a **wall-mounted broom holder**
- a **large-diameter PVC pipe clamp**
- an **electrical outlet wall box**

Hand-sketching did a lot of the work here — it was the quickest way to communicate an idea,
including across a language and office culture that was new to me. I also ran mechanical
tests on the wall-mounted clamp prototypes to confirm our material choices actually held to
spec before designs went back to the client.
